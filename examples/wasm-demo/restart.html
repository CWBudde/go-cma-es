Why population doubling

A local CMA-ES run is intentionally good at exploiting one basin. IPOP turns that strength into a global strategy by restarting with increasingly large populations, raising the chance that a generation spans useful basins.

Why the map is honest

Where a landscape has a closed-form lattice of local optima — Rastrigin's integer grid, Schaffer's rings at radius kπ — the line from a winner to its violet square names the basin it settled in, rather than reducing every restart to one final scalar.
