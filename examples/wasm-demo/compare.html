Why this is the test

A fixed circular proposal must choose between progress along the flat direction and overshooting the narrow direction. CMA-ES can rotate and stretch its proposal, turning a million-to-one objective into a much better conditioned search problem.

What “same” means

Both arms start at the same mean, use the same λ, σ₀, seed, generation count, and standard-normal draw order. The control performs the same log-weighted recombination but never adapts σ or covariance.
